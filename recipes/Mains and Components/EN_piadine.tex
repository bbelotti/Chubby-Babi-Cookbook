\recipe{Piadine \normalfont(Italian flatbread)}{Makes 3 piadine}{}{}

\subsection*{Ingredients}
\begin{ingredients}
	\item 1 dl water
	\item 180 g flour
	\item 1 tbsp milk
	\item 1 tbsp olive oil
	\item \sfrac{1}{2} tsp salt
\end{ingredients}

\medskip

% --- Preparation ---
In a bowl, mix together \textbf{all the ingredients}, adding the \textbf{flour} gradually.

Let the dough rest for 10 minutes (covered or wrapped in plastic in the refrigerator).

Divide the dough into 3 balls and roll them out. Cook in a skillet over medium-high heat for about 1 minute, until bubbles form.
