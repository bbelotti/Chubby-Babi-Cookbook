\recipe{Burger Buns}{Makes anywhere between 4 very large buns and 8 small ones.}{}{}

\begin{multicols}{2}
	\subsection*{Ingredients}
	\subsubsection*{Bread}
	\begin{ingredients}
		\item 450 g AP flour
		\item 235 ml water
		\item 1 egg
		\item 3 tbsp melted butter
		\item 3 tbsp granulated sugar
		\item 7 g dry yeast
		\item Salt
		\item Sesame seeds
	\end{ingredients}
	
		\columnbreak
	\subsection*{}
	\subsubsection*{Egg wash}
	\begin{ingredients}
		\item 1 egg
		\item 1 tbsp milk
	\end{ingredients}
\end{multicols}

\medskip

% --- Preparation ---
Put the \textbf{yeast} in the \textbf{water} and let it wake up\footnote{Add a pinch of flour and sugar to help. Wait a few minutes, until the surface foams.}, then add the \textbf{egg}, \textbf{butter}, \textbf{sugar}, and \textbf{salt}. Mix well while adding the \textbf{flour}. 
Let rise for 2h in a warm place\footnote{I usually leave it in the oven with the light on.}. 
Form the desired number of balls (4 to 8), set on an oven tray, cover, and let rise for another hour. 
Preheat the oven to 190°C, brush the buns with the well-mixed \textbf{egg} and \textbf{milk}, then bake for 15-20 minutes.
