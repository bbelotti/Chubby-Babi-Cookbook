\recipe{Pastry Cream\label{crema_pasticcera}}{}{}{}

\subsection*{Ingredients}
\begin{ingredients}
	\item 500 g (17.6 oz) whole milk
	\item 130 g (4.6 oz) sugar
	\item 7 egg yolks\footnote{For a lighter version, replace the 7 egg yolks with 2 egg yolks and 1 whole egg.}
	\item 40 g (1.4 oz) cornstarch
	\item 2 vanilla pods, or 6 g (0.21 oz) vanilla extract
	\item Zest of \sfrac{1}{2} lemon
\end{ingredients}

\medskip

% --- Preparation ---
If using \textbf{vanilla pods}, scrape out the seeds and cut the pods into small pieces.
Pour the \textbf{milk} into a saucepan, add half of the \textbf{sugar}, the \textbf{vanilla}, and the \textbf{lemon zest}.

Bring to a boil over medium-low heat.
Meanwhile, in a separate bowl, combine the \textbf{egg yolks} (and, if using it, the \textbf{egg}) with the other half of the \textbf{sugar} and the \textbf{cornstarch}.
Whisk until smooth and creamy.
As soon as the milk reaches a gentle boil, pour a little of it into the bowl with the egg mixture to temper it, then slowly pour the entire mixture back into the milk in a thin stream, whisking continuously.

After a few minutes of careful whisking, the cream will thicken, almost suddenly.
Remove it from the heat as soon as it reaches the desired consistency, immediately pour it into a cold container, and let it cool in the refrigerator\footnote{To prevent a skin from forming on the surface of the cream as it cools, press some plastic wrap against its surface.}.
