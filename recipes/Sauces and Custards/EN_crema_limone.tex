\recipe{Lemon Cream}{Double the recipe if using it to fill a tart.}{}{}

\subsection*{Ingredients}
\begin{ingredients}
	\item 1 egg
	\item 100 g sugar
	\item 1 tbsp cornstarch
	\item Juice and zest of 1 unwaxed lemon
	\item 100 ml cold water
\end{ingredients}

\medskip 

% --- Preparation ---
In a saucepan, vigorously whisk together \textbf{all the ingredients}.
Cook over low heat, stirring occasionally, until the cream has thickened.
The longer it cooks, the thicker and firmer the cream will become.
