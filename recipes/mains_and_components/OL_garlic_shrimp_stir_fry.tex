\recipe{Garlic Shrimp Stir Fry}{For 2-3 servings.}{}{}

\subsection*{Ingredients}
\begin{ingredients}
	\item Rice or other side of choice
	\item 6 garlic cloves, minced
	\item 60 ml (\sfrac{1}{4} cup) soy sauce
	\item 60 ml (\sfrac{1}{4} cup) water
	\item 2 tbsp light brown sugar
	\item 1 tbsp sesame oil
	\item 1 tbsp vegetable oil
	\item 1 tbsp chili sauce (like \textit{sambal oelek})
	\item 1 tbsp corn starch
	\item 1 red bell pepper, sliced
	\item Any other veggies of choice
	\item Shrimp or any other protein of choice\footnote{If making this dish with beef, chicken, or pork, consider \textit{velveting} the meat first: this simple and fast tenderizing step will give the meat that restaurant-like texture: for every 250 g (8 oz) of striped meat, add \sfrac{3}{4} tsp of baking soda, mix well, refrigerate for $\sim$20 minutes, rinse, and shake off the excess water before stir frying.}
\end{ingredients}

\medskip

% --- Preparation ---
Start by cooking the \textbf{rice} (or side of choice).
In a small bowl, combine the minced \textbf{garlic} with the \textbf{water}, \textbf{soy sauce}, \textbf{sugar}, \textbf{sesame oil}, \textbf{chili sauce}, and \textbf{corn starch}. 
Whisk until smooth.

In a large skillet, add the \textbf{vegetable oil} over medium-high heat, add the \textbf{bell pepper} and the other \textbf{veggies}, depending on their cooking time.
Sauté until softened ($\sim$5 minutes), then add the \textbf{shrimp} and cook until pink and opaque.

Pour the \textbf{sauce} into the skillet and cook until it thickens enough to coat the shrimp.
Serve over the rice.
