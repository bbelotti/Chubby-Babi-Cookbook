\recipe{Stroganoff Pasta}{Serves 4.}{}{}
\vspace*{-0.5 cm}
\begin{multicols}{2}
	\subsection*{Ingredients}
	\begin{ingredients}
		\item 1 kg sirloin or chuck steak
		\item 50 g (3 tbsp) soy sauce
		\item 35 g (2 tbsp) Worcestershire sauce (15 g for the marinade and 20 g for the sauce)
		\item 200 g or about 1 onion, finely diced
		\item 450 g button mushrooms, sliced
		\item 4--5 garlic cloves (minced or crushed)
		\item High-smoke-point vegetable oil
		\item Salt and black pepper
		\item 175 g (1 glass) dry white wine
		\item 25 g (1.5 tbsp) tomato paste
		\item 15 g (2 tbsp) flour
		\item 400 g beef stock, with 3 packets unflavoured powdered gelatin dissolved in it (optional)
		\item 25 g (1\sfrac{2}{3} tbsp) Dijon mustard
		\item 450 g pasta of your choice
		\item 65 g butter
		\item 75 g heavy cream
	\end{ingredients}
	
\end{multicols}

%\medskip
\vspace*{-0.2cm}

% --- Preparation ---
Tenderize (or \textit{Jaccard}) the \textbf{steak} by piercing it with a fork.
Place it in a bag with the \textbf{soy sauce} and 15 g of the \textbf{Worcestershire sauce}, then refrigerate for 15--20 minutes while preparing the remaining ingredients.

Pat the steak dry and heat some \textbf{oil} in a heavy-bottomed skillet (ideally cast iron). 
Add the steak, pressing firmly against the pan.
Cook over medium-high heat for 1.5 minutes on one side, or until well browned, then 2--3 minutes on the other side.
Transfer the meat to a plate and loosely wrap it in foil.

Add another splash of oil to the skillet, then add the \textbf{mushrooms} with a pinch of salt. 
Stir and deglaze\footnote{In this recipe, as in many others---particularly in French cuisine---deglazing is an essential step that builds a deep and complex flavor.} the pan with a wooden spoon.
Sauté for 4--5 minutes, until some of the mushrooms' moisture has evaporated.
Add the \textbf{onion} with another pinch of salt, stir, and cook for 3--4 minutes.
Add the \textbf{garlic} and cook for about 20 seconds, then pour in the \textbf{white wine}.
Deglaze thoroughly and, once the wine has reduced slightly, stir in the \textbf{tomato paste} and \textbf{flour}.
These will coat the bottom of the pan in about a minute.

Add the \textbf{beef stock} with the dissolved \textbf{gelatin}, deglaze, and bring to a boil. 
Stir in the \textbf{Dijon mustard}, the remaining \textbf{Worcestershire sauce}, and some \textbf{black pepper}.
Cover and let the sauce simmer gently for about 10 minutes, then stir in the \textbf{heavy cream}.
Meanwhile, cook the \textbf{pasta}. 
Slice the steak into thin strips and add it to the reduced sauce.
Toss everything together, ensuring the meat is heated through, and serve.
