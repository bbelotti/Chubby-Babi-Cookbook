\recipe{Burger Sauce}{}{}{}

\begin{multicols}{2}
	\subsection*{Ingredients}
	\begin{ingredients}
		\item 125 g mayonnaise
		\item 60 g ketchup
		\item 15 g shallot or onion
		\item 25 g of sriracha or chili
		\item 15 g Dijon mustard
		\item 60 g (=1 medium) pickle
		\item \sfrac{1}{2} garlic clove
		\item 2 g salt (a generous pinch)
		\item 2 g pepper
	\end{ingredients}
\end{multicols}

\medskip

% --- Preparation ---
Blend \textbf{all the ingredients} with a (hand-held) food processor and serve. 
Can be done without a food processor by finely mincing all the solid ingredients with a knife.
