\recipe{Sushi rice}{Makes enough for $\sim$8 rolls}{}{}

\begin{ingredients}
	\item 360 g (2 cups) raw sushi rice
	\item 60 g (\sfrac{1}{4} cup) rice vinegar
	\item 4 tsp sugar
	\item 1 tsp salt
\end{ingredients}

\medskip

% --- Preparation ---
Start cooking the \textbf{rice} at least 4 hours before you plan to start rolling your sushi.
While the rice cooks, mix \textbf{all other ingredients} together in a glass or bowl\footnote{Microwaving the mix for 10 seconds will help dissolve the salt and sugar.}.
Once the rice is done, mix in the liquid, stir well, and let the rice cool completely, covered.
