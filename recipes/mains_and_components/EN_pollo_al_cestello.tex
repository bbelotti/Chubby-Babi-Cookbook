\recipe{Chicken in a Basket}{The Attinghausen-style recipe. A.k.a. ``Devil's Chicken'' (\textemdash Bailee Z., lactose intolerant.) Serves 2.}{}{}

\begin{multicols}{2}
	\subsection*{Ingredients}
	
	\subsubsection*{For the marinade}
	\begin{ingredients}
		\item 1 whole chicken (1 kg)
		\item 2 dl sour cream
		\item 3 tbsp olive oil
		\item 1 tsp black pepper
		\item 2 tsp salt
		\item a pinch of dried garlic
	\end{ingredients}
	
	\subsubsection*{For the sauce}
	\begin{ingredients}
		\item 1 large or 2 small shallots
		\item 2 tbsp olive oil
		\item White wine, for deglazing
		\item 1 dl vegetable stock
		\item 2.5 dl heavy cream
		\item 1.5 tsp mustard
		\item 0.5 tsp Madras curry powder
		\item 0.5 tsp tarragon
		\item 60 g butter
		\item black pepper, to taste
		\item salt, to taste
	\end{ingredients}
	
	\subsubsection*{To serve}
	\begin{ingredients}
		\item French fries
		\item Cornichons
	\end{ingredients}
	
\end{multicols}

\vspace{-0.2 cm}

% --- Preparation ---
\textbf{Marinate} the \textbf{chicken} in the well-mixed marinade for at least 4 hours in the refrigerator, turning it occasionally.
Remove it from the refrigerator 30 minutes before roasting.
Roast the chicken in the center of the oven\footnote{Alternatively, directly on the oven rack. In this case, place a baking tray filled with water underneath the chicken to catch the dripping fat.} at 200°C for about 1 hour.
Cook for 20 minutes on each side, then switch to the grill setting for another 10 minutes per side.
If serving \textbf{French fries}, bake them during the last 20 minutes of the chicken's cooking time, then continue baking them for another 10 minutes while the chicken rests outside the oven.

For the sauce, sauté the \textbf{shallot} in the \textbf{olive oil} and deglaze with the \textbf{white wine}.
Once the wine has evaporated, add the \textbf{vegetable stock} and cook for 1 minute.
Add the \textbf{cream}, \textbf{curry powder}, \textbf{black pepper}, \textbf{tarragon}, and \textbf{mustard}.
Simmer gently for 10--15 minutes until slightly reduced (the butter will loosen it again), then stir in the \textbf{butter} and finally adjust the \textbf{salt} to taste.
If necessary, keep the sauce over very low heat, stirring occasionally, until the chicken is ready.
It should have a nice glossy appearance.
Serve half a chicken per person in a basket with the sauce, half a cornichon, and the French fries on the side. Eat strictly with your hands.
