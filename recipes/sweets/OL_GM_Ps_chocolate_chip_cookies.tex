\recipe{Grandma P's chocolate chip cookies}{Jordan's grandma's original recipe.}{}{}

\subsection*{Ingredients}
\begin{ingredients}
	\item 1 cup (200 g) brown sugar
	\item \sfrac{1}{2} cup (100 g) white sugar
	\item 1 cup (205 g) shortening\footnote{A shortening is any fat that is solid at room temperature and contains zero water. The original recipe calls for vegetable shortening (like \textit{Crisco}).} or butter, at room temperature.
	\item 1 tsp vanilla extract
	\item 2 eggs
	\item 2 cups (250 g) flour
	\item 1 tsp (6 g) salt
	\item 1 tsp (5 g) baking soda
	\item 2 cups (340 g) chocolate chips
\end{ingredients}

\medskip

% --- Preparation ---
Cream together the \textbf{brown} \& \textbf{white sugar}, the \textbf{shortening} (or butter), and the \textbf{vanilla extract}.
Mix in the \textbf{eggs}, then \textbf{all remaining ingredients}.

Scoop the dough onto a baking sheet\footnote{The same way you'd scoop ice cream.}, leaving enough space between each ball.
Bake at 340°F (170°C) for approximately 10 mins.
