\recipe{Pancakes}{Makes 8 pancakes.}{}{}

\subsection*{Ingredients}
\begin{ingredients}
	\item 1\sfrac{1}{2} cups (187 g) all-purpose flour
	\item 3\sfrac{1}{2} tsp (14 g) baking powder
	\item 1 tsp (6 g) salt
	\item 1 tbsp (12 g) sugar
	\item 1\sfrac{1}{2} cups (355 ml) milk
	\item 1 egg
	\item 3 tbsp (40 g) melted butter
\end{ingredients}

\medskip

% --- Preparation ---
Pour \textbf{all the powders} in a bowl and mix well. 
Make a hole in the middle and pour in \textbf{all the moist ingredients}. 
Make sure the eggs are well incorporated with the milk and butter before mixing to form a smooth batter.
Preheat a non-stick skillet on medium-low heat. 
Grease the skillet before pouring $\sim$\sfrac{1}{4} cup of batter for each pancake\footnote{As opposed to \nameref{crepes}, pancakes should be thick, and must not be spread on the pan after the batter is poured.}. As the pancake cooks, bubbles will form all over its raw surface.
Flip the pancake once its bottom reaches the desired level of browning\footnote{This can be checked by gently lifting the side of the pancake with a spatula.}.

Serve warm with butter and syrup, or with your preferred toppings.
