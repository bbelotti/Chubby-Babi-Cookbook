\recipe{Crêpes}{Sweet or savory. Makes 15 crêpes, each 20 cm (8 in) in diameter.}{}{}

\subsection*{Ingredients}
\begin{ingredients}
	\item 3 medium eggs
	\item 250 g (2 cups) flour
	\item 500 ml (2 cups) milk
	\item Butter, for greasing the pan
	\item 2 pinches of salt
	\item Sugar (optional)
\end{ingredients}

\medskip

% --- Preparation ---
Crack the \textbf{eggs} into a bowl, beat them with a fork, then add the \textbf{milk}, the \textbf{salt}, and, if desired, the \textbf{sugar}.
Whisk in the \textbf{flour} until the batter is smooth and free of lumps.
Heat a non-stick skillet over medium heat and pour in enough batter to make a crêpe of the desired size\footnote{If available, use a crêpe spreader to spread the batter evenly over the bottom of the pan. Otherwise, simply tilt the pan or use a spatula to distribute the batter.}.
Flip the crêpe as soon as it releases easily from the pan.
