\recipe{Banana Bread}{Makes one loaf.}{}{}
\label{banana_bread}

\subsection*{Ingredients}
\begin{ingredients}
	\item 3 or 4 very ripe bananas
	\item 110 g (\sfrac{1}{2} cup) unsalted butter at room temperature
	\item 150 g (\sfrac{3}{4} cup) granulated sugar
	\item 2 large eggs
	\item 188 g (1\sfrac{1}{2} cup) flour
	\item 1 tsp baking soda
	\item \sfrac{1}{2} tsp salt
	\item 1 tsp vanilla extract 
	\item 200 g (1-2 cups) filling of choice\footnote{E.g., chocolate chips, nuts, raisins, oats, etc., or any combination of these.}
\end{ingredients}

\medskip

% --- Preparation ---
Preheat the oven to 177°C (350°F), grease and flour a bread loaf pan.
In a mixing bowl, cream together the \textbf{butter} and the \textbf{sugar}.
Mash the \textbf{bananas} with a fork and add to the batter, along with the \textbf{eggs} and \textbf{vanilla}.
Mix well.

Separately, whisk together all the \textbf{dry ingredients}, and add to the mix.
Add the filling, mix well, and pour the batter into the bread pan.

Bake for 55-60 minutes, or until a toothpick comes out clean.
Let rest in the pan for 10-20 minutes before transferring to a cooling rack.
