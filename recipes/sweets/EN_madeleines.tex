\recipe{Madeleines}{Buttery and citrusy French muffins, my favorite breakfast along with croissants. Makes 24 madeleines.}{}{}

\subsection*{Ingredients}
\begin{ingredients}
	\item 1\sfrac{1}{2} cups (175 g) flour
	\item \sfrac{3}{4} cup (175 g) butter
	\item 3 eggs
	\item \sfrac{3}{4} cup (150 g) granulated sugar
	\item \sfrac{1}{2} tsp baking powder
	\item 2 pinches of salt
	\item Zest of 1 orange
	\item Zest of 1 lemon
	\item 2 tsp (10 g) honey
	\item 1 tsp vanilla extract
\end{ingredients}

\medskip

% --- Preparation ---
Melt the \textbf{butter} in the microwave and let it cool until lukewarm.
In a bowl, thoroughly mix \textbf{the sugar} and the \textbf{zests}.
Add the melted \textbf{butter}, mix, then add the \textbf{eggs} and \textbf{vanilla}.
Mix well\footnote{Do not beat the mixture, otherwise the madeleines will become too airy and light.} until obtaining a smooth and homogeneous batter.

Let the batter rest in the refrigerator for at least 1 hour.

Preheat the oven to 355°F (180°C), butter one or more madeleine molds, and divide the batter among 24 cavities.

Bake for 13 minutes.
The madeleines are ready when they are golden brown and have formed their characteristic hump.
