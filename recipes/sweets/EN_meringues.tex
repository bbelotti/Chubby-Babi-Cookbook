\recipe{Meringues\label{meringues}}{Yields about 20 meringues.}{}{}

\subsection*{Ingredients}
\begin{ingredients}
	\item 4 egg whites
	\item 300 g (1.5 cups) granulated sugar
\end{ingredients}

\medskip

% --- Preparation ---
Preheat the oven to 120°C (250°F), and prepare the \textbf{egg whites} at room temperature. 
Start by beating the whites at medium-low speed. 
When they begin to foam, if available, add a few drops of acid (lemon juice or white vinegar\footnote{Acid aids in retaining air bubbles, thus making it easier to achieve a firm whip. For geologists, 10\% HCl is not a recommended alternative.}).

As soon as the beaters start leaving trails in the foam, increase the speed to medium-high and sprinkle in \sfrac{1}{3} of the \textbf{sugar}.

Wait 1 to 2 minutes for the sugar to fully incorporate, then add the second third.

Add the final third when the meringue turns bright white and starts building volume.

Whip the meringue at maximum speed until it holds a firm stiff peak.

Shape\footnote{Piping is not necessary; simply dollop small shapes using a spoon.} the meringues onto a baking sheet lined with parchment paper, then bake for 20 minutes at 120°C (250°F), followed by 1 hr at 100°C (210°F).
