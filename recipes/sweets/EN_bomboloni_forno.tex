\recipe{Baked Bomboloni \noGF}{Makes 12 bomboloni.}{}{}

\subsection*{Ingredienti}
\begin{ingredients}
	\item 300 g (10.5 oz) flour (divided into 120 g (4.2 oz) + 180 g (6.3 oz))
	\item 50 g (1.75 oz) sugar ($+$ extra for dusting)
	\item 1 pinch of salt
	\item 7 g (0.25 oz / 1 packet) instant dry yeast
	\item 150 ml (5 fl oz) warm milk
	\item 15 g (0.5 oz / 1 tbsp) melted butter ($+$ a little extra for brushing)
	\item 2 egg yolks
	\item \sfrac{1}{2} tsp vanilla extract
	\item Filling of choice (\textit{\nameref{crema_pasticcera}} (p. \pageref{crema_pasticcera}), jam, ...)
\end{ingredients}

\medskip

% --- Preparation ---
In a large bowl, add 120 g (4.2 oz) of \textbf{flour}, the \textbf{sugar}, the \textbf{yeast}, and a pinch of \textbf{salt}, and mix with a whisk. 
Add the \textbf{warm milk}, \textbf{melted butter}, \textbf{egg yolks}, and \textbf{vanilla}. 
Mix until smooth, then cover and let rest for 10 minutes. 

Add the remaining \textbf{flour} and knead the dough until smooth and no longer sticky ($\sim$5 minutes), then cover and let rise until it doubles in size\footnote{Place in the oven (turned off) with the light on for 45--90 minutes.}.

Flour your work surface and the dough, then roll it out with a rolling pin to a thickness of $\sim$1.5 cm (0.6 in). 
Use a glass or a round cutter to cut out $\sim$12 bomboloni from the rolled-out dough, and place them on a baking sheet lined with parchment paper.
Cover them with a lid or a dry, floured towel and let them rest in the oven with the light on for 30--40 minutes. 

Once the bomboloni are nicely puffed up, bake them in the center of the (preheated) oven at 190°C (375°F) for 10--15 minutes, until golden brown. 

Once baked and cooled, fill them with your \textbf{filling} of choice, brush them with \textbf{melted butter}, and roll them in \textbf{sugar}\footnote{Or dust them with powdered sugar.}.
